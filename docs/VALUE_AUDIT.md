### 5g. Selection bias and missing data in this section

- Settled selected legs: **21** (needs 50). Everything above is from 4 calendar days.
- The reviewer saw each pick's model probability, its backtest win-rate record and the current price, and preferred high-probability winner picks. Selected and excluded picks therefore differ in sport, market and probability by construction; raw win rates are not comparable, which is why 5a also shows outcomes relative to the model and sportsbook probabilities.
- Many exclusions are mechanical (game locked by an earlier ticket, line moved, no price). They are not evidence about judgement.
- Reviews exist only for the hours the workflow ran; forecasts never reviewed are not in the pool. Before 8 Nov there is no per-forecast decision, only the selected legs.
- Team totals and the NHL regulation market have no stored price, so their excluded picks have no sportsbook comparison and no return.
- The review instructions and helper code changed repeatedly during these days (5f); this is not one fixed selection rule.
- No stake, placed bet or payout is recorded. Ticket returns are hypothetical at the stored price at send time; the sportsbook's actual parlay price can differ.
