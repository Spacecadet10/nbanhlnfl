## 0. Limits, stated first
- H1–H7 were found on the same 297 cards (Oct 17–Nov 10) they were counted on, out of many looks. They are candidates,
  not facts. Most will probably fail on new games.
- Test A power: with the sportsbook expecting about 75% for the named picks, a one-sided 5% test on 100 picks needs about
  7 more wins than expected (about +7 points). Passing under the power de-vig as well (which gives favourites a higher
  expectation than the proportional one) makes it stricter still. A real but small edge will read as "drop".
- Test B power (ROUGH: normal approximation, group sizes assumed, not measured): the test uses only picks that are not
  in both groups. If about 30 of the 100 named picks differ from their card's baseline pick and about 170 baseline picks
  are not named picks (cards naming nothing, or naming another pick), a hit has a spread of about 0.45, the standard
  error of the difference is about 0.09, and the named-only picks need a hit rate roughly 15 points above the
  baseline-only picks to pass. Baseline picks are the card's sportsbook favourites, so beating them on raw hit rate is a
  hard bar by construction. Leaving shared picks out of the shuffle means fewer picks are tested, so the test has less
  power than its total group sizes suggest; the hit rates of the full groups are reported beside it. Expect "drop"
  unless the difference is large.
- Only NBA winner and NHL puck-line picks can reach the floor (section 4). Every other market is recorded, never named.
- NHL: many puck-line picks carry a lineup flag before the goalie is confirmed (about two hours before the start is often
  too early), and flagged picks are never named. NHL may name few picks and end as "too few games" (section 7).
- Assumptions of the tests: game outcomes are independent given the quotes; the de-vigged quote is the sportsbook's fair
  expectation; the counted cards are an unselected stream (every sent card is recorded). If the recorder misses cards,
  that is reported, not repaired.
- NFL and soccer: record only, name nothing (section 8). Win counts only; no profit claim follows from any result.

## 1. Units, time and "new games"
- **T0** = the committer timestamp of the git commit that first adds this file
  (`git log --diff-filter=A --format=%cI -- docs/preregistration/best-pick-2026-11-11-v4.md`).
- **Card** = one NBA or NHL game card that the delivery job (`app.telegram.run_once`) actually sent (`deliver_card`
  returned `sent`). Send time = the report's `as_of` used to build that card.
- **Counted card**: all of
  1. sent at or after T0, for a game whose `tipoff_at` is after T0;
  2. recorded live at send time by a recorder whose stored `prereg_sha256` equals the sha256 of this file;
  3. recorded at or after the recorder's **independent review** against this document was completed and written in
     HANDOFF.md, and the reviewed recorder was released. Rows recorded before that are excluded, whole.
  If a game has more than one sent card, only the earliest-sent one counts. Nothing is reconstructed afterwards: a card
  without a live record does not count (no backdating).
- Information used = what the card job could see at send time: the card's own report
  (`RealProvider(store, as_of).ranked_picks(...)`, the object the card is built from), quote rows with `retrieved_at` at
  or before the pick's `generated_at`, `games` rows (the schedule), and `lineup.facts(conn, sport, games, as_of)`. No
  result, quote or report retrieved after send time.
- **Same-game picks** = all picks with the same `game_id` in that report at send time (every market the report has for
  the game), not only those shown on the card.

## 2. Candidates (per card)
A pick of the card's game is a **candidate** when all hold:
1. market is in NBA {winner, spread, total} or NHL {winner, spread, total, reg3} (team totals excluded: no hypothesis,
   no sportsbook price);
2. `probability` (app) and `market_prob` (sportsbook, proportional de-vig, as `ranked_picks` returns it) are both present;
3. `lineup.review_flags(sport, pick, facts[game_id])` returns an empty list. "Lineup facts unavailable" or any exception
   counts as a flag. **A pick with a lineup flag is never named.**
4. Not AVOID on its exact live record: excluded when `percentage_history.rank` is not None and
   `percentage_history.losses > percentage_history.wins` (the card's own AVOID). Only the live exact-percentage record
   (`threshold_overview.percentage_record`, as of send time) is used; the recomputed past-seasons `history` and any
   tuned season never count.

All percentages below are **displayed** values, `threshold_overview.displayed_percentage(p)` (one decimal, half-up),
compared as decimals. "Book %" = displayed `market_prob` of the picked side; "app %" = displayed `probability`.

## 3. Score (H1–H7 only; each item +1, -1 or 0; score = sum)
Sides are `percentage_history.side` (`a` = home, `b` = away for winner/spread/reg3; `mid` = draw for reg3).

NBA winner
- H1: book % >= 72.0 → +1; book % <= 58.0 → -1.
- H2: app % < book % → +1; app % - book % >= 5.6 → -1.
- H3: the same game's NBA spread pick is on the other side → +1; the same side → -1; no spread pick → 0.
- H4 (sportsbook moved toward the pick; NBA only): quotes come from table `odds_quotes` (`market='moneyline'`, column
  `home_prob_devig`; NHL moneylines would be `market_quotes` `market='winner'` `p_a_devig`, not used here). "Now" quote =
  the row the app's `_winner_quote_prob(game_id, generated_at)` selects (latest `retrieved_at` at or before the pick's
  `generated_at`; ties `price_label='current'` first, then highest `quote_id`). "Opening" quote = the row of the same
  table, same `game_id`, same market and **same `provider` as the "now" quote** with the earliest `retrieved_at` at or
  before `generated_at` (ties: `price_label='open'` first, then lowest `quote_id`). "Opening" means the first quote this
  app stored, not the market's true opening price. movement = displayed picked-side % of "now" minus displayed
  picked-side % of "opening". movement >= +3.0 → +1; movement <= -3.0 → -1; otherwise 0. No "now" quote, no
  same-provider opening quote, or opening = now → 0.

NHL spread (puck line)
- H5a: picked team equals the same game's NHL winner pick → +1; different → -1; no winner pick → 0.
- H5b: the puck-line pick's own app % >= 67.0 → +1.
- H5c: the picked team (of the puck-line pick) played the previous day → -1.
- Source and ambiguity: the owner brief reads "H5 NHL puck line: on team app also picks to win 28/32; otherwise 64/117.
  Better at app 67%+, worse when picked team on back-to-back (10/21 vs 82/128)." "App 67%+" could mean the puck-line
  pick's % or the same game's winner %; it is listed under the puck line, so it is read as the puck-line pick's own app %.
  The owner's first prompt said "worse when the picked team is on a back-to-back", so H5c is the puck-line pick's team,
  not either team.

Totals (NBA and NHL)
- H6: either team played the previous day → -1. (No positive item.)

NHL reg3
- H7: the same game's NHL winner pick has app % >= 60.0 and the reg3 pick is the same team → +1; otherwise (below 60.0,
  a draw pick, the other team, no winner pick) → -1.

NBA spread, NHL winner: no hypothesis, score 0.

"Played the previous day": the team has a `games` row (home or away), status not `postponed` or `canceled`, whose
`tipoff_at` falls on the America/Toronto calendar date one day before this game's America/Toronto date.

## 4. The named pick, the floor and ties
- **Floor: score >= 2.** Below it the card's best pick is "none stands out".
- Named pick = the candidate with score >= 2, ordered by: higher score; higher book %; higher app %; market order
  winner, spread, total, reg3; lower `forecast_id`.
- Consequence (by construction, not by data): only NBA winner (max +4) and NHL spread (max +2) can be named.

## 5. Baseline: "highest sportsbook % on the card"
For **every** counted card (named or not): among the same candidates (section 2, same lineup and AVOID exclusions), the
one with the highest book % (proportional de-vig, displayed); ties: higher app %, market order, lower `forecast_id`. A card with no candidate has no
baseline.

## 6. Grading and expectations
- Each named and baseline pick is graded by the app's own settlement of its recorded `forecast_id` (`markets.settle` with
  that forecast's line, as `scripts/best_picks.py` does), read at evaluation time.
- Decided = win or loss. Push, void, unscorable: not decided, excluded and counted. A pick with no final grade 7 days
  after its `tipoff_at` is excluded and reported.
- Two expectations per pick, both from the **same quote** the recorded `market_prob` came from (the recorder stores that
  quote's table, `quote_id` and American prices):
  - **Proportional**: the recorded `market_prob` (unrounded); same as `app.odds.devig_two_way` for two outcomes:
    q_i = r_i / sum_j r_j, with r = `app.odds.american_to_implied(price)` for every outcome of the quote (2 or 3).
  - **Power**: q_i = r_i^k with k solved so that sum_j r_j^k = 1, by bisection on k in [0.5, 5] until the sum is within
    1e-12 of 1, at most 200 iterations (no existing helper; this definition is binding). The picked side's q is the
    expectation. If sum_j r_j^k - 1 does not change sign on [0.5, 5], or 200 iterations do not reach 1e-12, the pick has
    no power expectation: it is left out of Test A's power calculation and of the reported power-excess figures, and
    counted and reported. It stays in Test A's proportional calculation and in Test B.
- Excess of a decided pick = (1 if win else 0) - proportional expectation.

## 7. Test and decision (per sport, NBA and NHL separately)
- **Evaluation set (named)**: the first 100 named picks, in send order, that are decided. Evaluated **once**, when the
  100th of them is decided and every earlier named pick is final or past its 7-day limit.
- **Baseline group**: the decided baseline picks of all counted cards of that sport sent no later than the card of the
  100th named pick (cards that name nothing included; a card whose named pick is its baseline pick contributes to both
  groups).
- **Test A (vs the sportsbook's own expectation)**: W = wins in the named set. For each expectation (proportional,
  power): one-sided exact Poisson-binomial p-value P(X >= W), X = sum of independent Bernoulli(q_i). Under the power
  expectation, W and the q_i are taken over the named picks that have a power expectation (section 6). **Pass only if
  p < 0.05 under both.**
- **Test B (decisive; the owner's criterion: hit rate vs the highest-sportsbook-% pick)**: a pick (same `forecast_id`)
  that is in both the named set and the baseline group is removed from both groups before the test. On what remains,
  statistic D = hit rate (wins / decided) of the named-only picks minus hit rate of the baseline-only picks. One-sided
  permutation test: pool the remaining 0/1 results, reassign group labels at random keeping both remaining group sizes,
  100,000 reassignments with numpy `default_rng(20260930)` (each one `rng.permutation` of the pooled array, named-only
  values first in send order, then baseline-only in send order; the first n_named_only values of each permutation form
  the named group); p = (1 + #{D_perm >= D}) / (1 + 100,000). Pass if p < 0.05. **Fail if either remaining group is
  empty.**
- **Reported only, not deciding**: the excess-over-book comparison, named set vs baseline group (mean excess of each and
  their difference), under the proportional and under the power expectation; and the hit rates of the full named set
  and full baseline group (shared picks included).
- **Keep** (the rule may then be proposed for the card; still needs review and the owner's go): Test A **and** Test B
  pass **and** the full named set's hit rate (wins / decided) is at least the full baseline group's hit rate (both full
  groups, shared picks included). Anything else → **drop** for that sport.
- **Deadline**: if a sport has not completed its named evaluation set by 2027-06-30 23:59Z, the result is "too few
  games" and the rule is dropped for that sport.
- Reported with the decision, not deciding: W/100 with Wilson 95% interval, mean proportional and power expectations,
  D, both full and both remaining group sizes, shared picks, picks without a power expectation, cards counted, named, excluded by lineup flag, by AVOID, pushes/voids, picks past the 7-day
  limit, cards sent without a record.

## 8. NFL and soccer
Steps D/E have not given either sport a rule. Their cards are recorded with the full picture (every pick, app %, book %
when present, exact live record) and **no pick is named**. Any rule for them needs its own preregistration, committed
before its own new games.

## 9. Integrity
- **No interim looks.** Before a sport's evaluation set is complete, reports show only counts (cards recorded, named,
  decided); no wins, losses, excess or rates of named or baseline picks.
- No change to the rule, floor, tie-breaks, markets, tests, alpha, n, seed or deadline after T0. **No amendment, at any
  time, may consult any game result after T0.** No subgroup, other market or other floor is promoted from these games;
  they may only seed a new preregistration whose own counting starts after its own commit.
- The recorder must be reviewed independently against this document before it goes live (section 1). A recorder defect
  found later: every record written by the defective version is excluded, whole, decided and written in HANDOFF.md
  before any result of those rows is read.
- The history line (`Past seasons, recomputed, model line`) and any season a model was tuned or selected on never feed
  the rule, the AVOID check or the tests.
- H8 is not used. The book-% tie-break is a neutral ordering choice made here.
