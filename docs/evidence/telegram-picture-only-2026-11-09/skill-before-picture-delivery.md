## Apply and challenge the selection

- Review winners, spreads, totals, regulation and team totals where available. Use only genuinely recorded pending forecasts within T−120 and before scheduled start.
- Compare the app ranking with your selection. Explain differences using actual forecast IDs. Preserve probabilities and picked sides. Do not manufacture independent AI percentages or reverse losing picks.
- Apply the November 6 all-market comparison process consistently. The app strong flag, 30-settled threshold, proven-market-skill label and verified positive price edge are not mandatory ticket gates. Review their evidence and disclose limitations, but do not reject an otherwise supported combination solely for lacking these labels or unverified prices. Compare all available markets and explain the strongest distinct-game selection. Exact-percentage records inform the review with their actual sample sizes. They do not by themselves establish a reliable probability. The November 6 archive does not demonstrate an automatic reversal strategy.
- Keep historical bands, current live records and exact-percentage records separate. Include sample sizes. Yesterday's successful tickets are examples of the process, not a validated selection rule.
- Weigh every eligible market on five things together (owner decision and selection audit, Nov 8 evening): the app probability; the app recommendation (game_rank: the app ranks by the record's lower bound, i.e. by hit rate, not price); the relevant record of similar picks (win rate, sample size, 95% interval, history vs season, kept separate from the exact-percentage record); evidence quality (the app's tested/untested notes in `assessment.app_note`, bookmaker vs model line, skill_proven); and the exact stored price (`assessment.price`, `break_even`, `model_gap`). Every pick carries `assessment` with these fields side by side. Read them as facts, not verdicts: a negative model_gap means the price asks for a higher win probability than the app estimates (a warning, not proof of a loss); a positive gap is not a value claim, because the estimate may be wrong and a past group of similar picks does not establish today's probability. `record_minus_break_even` compares that group's interval with the price's break-even probability; it is not a measure of accuracy, and a small sample can score well on it without being trustworthy. Do not choose the highest percentage by default, and do not add a heavily priced favorite just because it keeps the ticket's hit percentage high: it multiplies risk for little payout. Judge each complete candidate ticket with `sh /Users/andry/nba-predictor-delivery/scripts/release_tickets.sh assess --plan <plan.json>` (payout at stored prices, break-even, model estimate, each leg's own record, legs where the sportsbook implies a higher chance than the model, unpriced legs, legs without a record, cited by leg number; no ticket-level historical range is computed because multiplying past intervals does not give today's ticket a win-probability interval) and compare it with its alternatives. Every ticket needs `payout_assessment`: one or two sentences on why its payout justifies its uncertainty; the helper refuses a ticket without it. No market-variety, ticket-count, ticket-size or positive-gap requirement and no template: send a pair, a larger ticket or none as the evidence supports. These are uncertainties to weigh case by case, never automatic exclusions: a market with no live record yet (including all NFL markets today), a market the app labels untested or no-skill, a model line, and a large disagreement with the sportsbook. A large disagreement warrants investigation (for example injury, lineup or goalie news the app does not use); it is not evidence of value or of missing information by itself. Fewer tickets is not itself better. Allow zero to six tickets per review, each with two to six legs from distinct games. Keep different games within each ticket. Justified variations in the same batch may share the identical forecast for a core game. Explain the overlap and combined exposure. Never repeat an identical ticket. Later batches may share an identical forecast for a core game within the same Toronto day, with combined exposure disclosed; conflicting forecasts for that game are rejected. Original sent tickets stay immutable. Build no ticket when support is insufficient.
- Each pick carries `quote_check` (latest stored sportsbook line/price by snapshot time). `review.line_changed` lists forecasts whose bookmaker line moved since the forecast; they are excluded (their probability belongs to a line no longer shown) and the helper refuses them. The ticket text shows the latest stored price per leg and its time; it is not an executable price. - Verify offered prices for the exact line when available. Never label model-generated team totals as bookmaker offers. Disclose missing prices. If executable slips are requested, skip lines whose availability cannot be confirmed.
- Assess correlation and common model errors. Multiplying probabilities for distinct games is only an independence approximation. Do not multiply same-game legs.
- Challenge unnecessary overlap, thin evidence, ambiguous Minnesota names, side/line transcription, source age and games already past start. Record why each leg survived review and why tempting alternatives were omitted.

## Review sent results

Use app.tickets settle (or preflight) before selection each run. Read the archived plan and delivery receipt. It grades immutable forecast IDs at their original lines, including pushes and voids, records every leg and appends corrections without overwriting old grades. Pending/unavailable legs stay visible. Suggested tickets have no assumed profit. Legacy v2 batches are graded but their already-reviewed results are not re-notified automatically. Preserve losing legs. Separate distinct legs, tickets, overlapping proposals and bets the user actually placed. Calculate profit only with known stakes, prices and settlement rules. Record demonstrated skill issues and narrow proposed improvements. Do not optimize rules on yesterday's outcomes.

End each invocation that ran a review or hit an operational failure by updating HANDOFF.md with actual verification, delivery status, limitations and next task. Trigger checks with review.due false add nothing.

## User-placed combinations

Required fields: slip_id, source, stake_usd, legs (exact selections); optional return_usd. This records user-provided settlement separately from AI suggestions. ## Complete-slate review

Continue reviews through the remaining slate whenever review.due is true. Take a fresh snapshot and review every newly available pregame forecast across all supported sports and markets. Compare against earlier selections and send only new justified combinations. Use tickets=[] and helper check to archive a review when no ticket is justified. No fixed staking template or daily allocation cap applies. Never describe the first batch as the whole day's best slate. Track unopened forecast windows as pending coverage and reassess when they open.

Every review requires review.forecasts_reviewed: a mapping of "sport:forecast_id" to {"decision":"candidate" or "excluded", "reason":"actual evidence-based assessment"}. Cover EVERY eligible pregame forecast in the snapshot and any newly published eligible forecasts detected at validation. The helper refuses incomplete coverage and selected legs marked excluded. A newer publication requires re-review. review.combination_comparison is required even for tickets=[]; save useful later alternatives in that reasoning without assigning new stakes. Show coverage counts and missing sports in the review summary. Presence checks cannot establish the quality of the AI judgment; reasons must reflect actual markets, sample sizes, prices and uncertainties.
