API Reference

Choose your integration

Five purpose-built APIs — each scoped to a distinct integration type. Pick the one that matches your use case and get started.

Trading API
Orders, odds & wallet management
Place and manage limit orders, access real-time odds and market data, and manage your wallet. Built for algorithmic traders and liquidity providers.
Algorithmic traders & LPs
Market Data API
Read-only market & event data
Read-only access to ProphetX market and event data including odds and pricing. Built for partners looking to display markets without placing trades.
Display partners
Parlay API
Quote & confirm parlay pricing
Quote and confirm parlay pricing for ProphetX users. Receive RFQ requests via WebSocket, respond with pricing, and confirm execution. Built for third-party parlay service providers.
Parlay service providers
ISV API
Embed ProphetX in your platform
Embed ProphetX markets into your own platform. Supports end-user onboarding, KYC, deposits, withdrawals, market orders, and parlays all within your own UI.
ISVs & platform operators
Direct Link API
Link accounts & place orders
Link a user's ProphetX account to your platform, then place orders directly from the client using their own credentials — your backend never touches user funds or keys. Built for odds aggregators and platform integrators.
Direct Link aggregators
