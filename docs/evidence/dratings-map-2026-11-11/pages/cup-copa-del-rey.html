Predictions Methodology

The Copa del Rey is the top domestic cup in the country of Spain. The tournament comprises of all the teams in the top five divisions of Spanish football: La Liga, La Liga 2, and the three divisions that follow. All of our projections for the games are based on the result after 90 minutes. All games will not be listed as we only have data on teams from the top leagues.
